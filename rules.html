Server Rules

All players are expected to follow these rules. Violations may result in a ban or rejection of your whitelist application.

01

Respect

No harassment, hate speech or targeted abuse. Treat every player the way you'd want to be treated.

02

Fair Play

No cheats, exploits or unauthorized clients. Any advantage gained unfairly ruins the experience for everyone.

03

Community

Keep chat and gameplay suitable for the private SMP. No spamming, trolling or disruptive behaviour.

04

Staff Decisions

Use support tickets for appeals and complaints rather than public arguments. Staff have the final say.

05

Griefing

Do not destroy, steal from or tamper with other players' builds without explicit permission.

06

No Teaming to Remove Players

Co-ordinated targeting of one player to eliminate them is not allowed. Lifesteal is competitive, not personal.
