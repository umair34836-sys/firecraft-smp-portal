FIRECRAFT SMP

Server Rules

FireCraft is a private whitelisted Lifesteal SMP. Every player on this server is hand-picked and trusted. These rules protect that trust.

🚫 No Griefing ❌ No Hacking 🤝 Respect Everyone 🔒 Whitelist Only ❤️ Lifesteal Rules
Consequences: Permanent Ban Unforgivable — no appeal Kick / Temp Ban Serious violation Warning First offence, correctable
❤️
This is a Lifesteal SMP. Killing another player permanently steals one of their max hearts. Dying to another player costs you one max heart. Reach zero max hearts and you are eliminated from the server. This is by design — but it comes with specific rules to keep competition fair and fun.

Everything else is vanilla survival. No TPA, no /home — travel via the nether highway.

🤝
Respect & Community
How we treat each other on the server, in chat, and on Discord.
R01

No Harassment or Hate Speech

Do not target, insult, or demean any player — in chat, Discord, or any other communication connected to FireCraft. This includes slurs, threats, and prolonged targeted mockery.

Permanent Ban
R02

Keep Chat Appropriate

No spam, excessive caps, offensive roleplay, or content unsuitable for a mixed-age community. Light banter and competition talk is fine — know the line.

Example: trash-talking after a fair kill = ok. Sustained personal abuse = not ok.
Warning
R03

Respect Builds and Claimed Space

Do not build directly on top of or inside another player's established area without their permission. Maintain a reasonable visual distance from existing bases. This is a persistent world — respect what others have built.

Warning → Kick
⚔️
Fair Play & Anti-Cheat
The game must be played as intended. No shortcuts, no unfair advantages.
R04

No Hacked Clients or Cheats

Zero tolerance. X-ray, kill aura, speed hacks, auto-clickers, fly hacks, or any modification that gives a gameplay advantage not available to all players. Vanilla client, Optifine/Iris, and approved performance mods only.

Allowed: Optifine, Sodium, Iris, Litematica (no build). Not allowed: X-ray packs, Wurst, impact client.
Permanent Ban
R05

No Exploiting Bugs or Duping

If you discover a game bug or exploit that gives an unfair advantage, report it. Do not use it. Item duplication ruins the economy and the survival experience for everyone.

Found a dupe glitch? Open a ticket. Using it? That's a ban.
Permanent Ban
R06

No Lag Machines or Server Disruption

Do not build contraptions designed to crash or severely lag the server. Efficient farms are fine — intentionally destructive redstone or entity spam is not. TPS affects every player on the server.

Permanent Ban
🏚️
Griefing & Stealing
Builds and storage are permanent. Destroying or stealing breaks the entire foundation of a private SMP.
R07

No Griefing

Do not destroy, damage, or significantly alter another player's build without their explicit permission. This applies to bases, farms, public infrastructure, and the nether highway. What you destroy stays destroyed — and so does your whitelist.

Breaking one block by accident: apologize and fix it. Destroying a player's base: permanent ban.
Permanent Ban
R08

No Stealing from Chests or Storage

Do not take items from another player's chests, barrels, or shulker boxes. The only exception is unlocked community chests explicitly labeled as free-to-take. Shops run on an honor system — theft breaks that trust.

Public farms with a sign saying "take freely" = fine. Any other player's storage = off limits.
Permanent Ban
R09

Do Not Loot Death Chests Without Permission

If a player dies, their items belong to them. Do not take from a death site unless the player has given you permission or explicitly abandoned the items. In Lifesteal, you already earned a heart — don't also loot the chest.

Temp Ban
❤️
Lifesteal Rules
Specific rules for the heart mechanic. Competition must stay fair and fun — not personal.
R10

No Coordinated Targeting to Eliminate One Player

Forming a group specifically to hunt down and eliminate a single player — removing all their hearts — is not allowed. Alliances are fine. Ganging up on someone to get them removed from the server is not. Lifesteal is competitive, not a tool for harassment.

2v2 PvP fight = fine. 5 players coordinate to kill one specific player over and over until elimination = ban.
Permanent Ban
R11

No Trapping Players for Heart Farming

Do not lure players into inescapable traps designed to kill them repeatedly to farm their hearts. Traps in a base during a raid are fine. Bait traps designed to farm eliminations are not.

Permanent Ban
R12

PvP Consent in Safe Zones

The area immediately around spawn is a no-PvP zone. If you are in someone's base that is clearly marked as a safe zone, no PvP. Outside those areas, PvP is always on — this is Lifesteal. Prepare accordingly.

The nether highway is PvP-enabled. Take precautions when travelling.
Kick → Temp Ban
🔒
Whitelist & Privacy
FireCraft is private by design. Keeping it that way is everyone's responsibility.
R13

Do Not Share the Server IP

The IP address and any direct connection details are for whitelisted players only. Do not post them publicly, share them on Discord, or give them to anyone not on the whitelist. Every new member goes through the application process — no exceptions.

Permanent Ban
R14

Do Not Invite Players Without Admin Approval

If you want to invite someone, direct them to the application form. You are welcome to vouch for them in your application review — but the decision is admin's. Bypassing the vetting process puts the whole community at risk.

Temp Ban
R15

One Account Per Player

Each real person gets one whitelisted account. Using alt accounts to avoid a ban, to gain extra resources, or to spy on other players is not allowed.

Permanent Ban
📋
Staff & Appeals
How to raise concerns, appeal decisions, and handle disputes.
R16

Use Tickets — Not Public Arguments

If you have a complaint, a ban appeal, or a report against another player — open a support ticket. Public arguments in chat or Discord about bans and punishments are not allowed and will not be entertained. Tickets are the only official channel.

Banned unfairly? Open a ticket. Saw someone griefing? Open a ticket with screenshots.
Warning
R17

Staff Decisions Are Final

Admins and moderators have the final say on all rule interpretations and punishments. You may appeal once through a ticket. Repeatedly contesting a decision, or pressuring staff publicly, results in your appeal being closed.

Kick
R18

Report Bugs and Problems

If you find a server issue, a bug, or suspicious behaviour from another player — report it. Open a ticket or use the /report command in-game. You keep the community safe by speaking up.
