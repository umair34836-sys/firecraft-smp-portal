AFTER YOU JOIN

Your First Day on FireCraft

🎰 Spin the Power Wheel

On first login, a custom animation plays and you receive one of 11 unique elemental powers — Fire, Ice, Wind, Dark, Lightning, Trader, Rift, Warden, Wither, Dragon, or Gravity. Read the full powers guide to know what each one does.

❤️ Lifesteal Begins

You start with 10 hearts. Kill a player to gain +1 max heart. Die to a player and lose 1 max heart. Reach 0 hearts and you are eliminated from the server. Every fight carries real stakes.

🐾 Tame Your First Companion

Hold the right taming item and right-click a mob to start a taming session. A Boss Bar timer appears — feed the mob the required number of times before it runs out. You can have up to 3 companions. They level up to 100 and fight alongside you.

🛡️ Craft Counter Armor

Each of the 5 custom armor sets counters specific powers. Ember Guard vs Fire Power (50% reduction), Frost Plate vs Ice & Wind (50%), Void Weave vs Dark & Rift (50%), Warden Carapace vs Warden Power (60%), Dragon Scale vs all powers (40% + 15 cap).

JOINING FAQ

Common Questions

Can cracked / non-premium accounts join?

FireCraft requires a legitimate purchased Minecraft Java account. Cracked clients are not supported to keep the server secure and the community trustworthy.

How long does the application review take?

Staff typically reviews within 24–48 hours. During busy periods it may take a little longer. Check your status in My Status — do not spam the support ticket.

My application was rejected — what now?

Open a support ticket with the "Whitelist / Application" category and explain your situation. You can also reach out directly on Discord.

Is there an age requirement?

No strict age requirement, but FireCraft expects mature and respectful behaviour. Applications that show maturity are more likely to be approved.

Can I invite a friend?

You can vouch for a friend in your application, but they still need to apply themselves. Do not share the server IP with non-whitelisted players — see Rule R13 and R14.

What is the server IP after I'm approved?

The server address is play.firecraft.fun:20011, Java Edition only. It will also appear in your My Status page once you are approved.
