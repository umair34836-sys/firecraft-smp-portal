Complete Guide to All 11 Custom Powers

Every elemental power in FireCraft SMP — active abilities, passives, PvP combos, and the best counter-armor for each.

By FireCraft Staff · Oct 8, 2026 · 8 min read

When you first join FireCraft SMP, a custom spin-wheel animation plays and you receive one of 11 unique elemental powers. That power defines how you fight, how you survive, and how other players will try to counter you.

This guide covers every power: what it does, how to use it in PvP, and which custom armor counters it.

⚡ How Powers Work

Every power gives an active ability (SNEAK + RIGHT CLICK) and a permanent passive. Your power is stored in a Shard item. Kill a player in PvP → steal their Shard. Die to a mob or environmental damage → Shard returns to you on respawn.

SPIN-ELIGIBLE · 10 POWERS

Standard Powers

These 10 powers can be assigned via the first-join spin wheel.

🔥

Fire

Shard: Blaze Rod
Standard
ActiveShoot a fireball in the direction you're facing
PassiveEvery melee hit sets the target on fire
PvP Tip: Open fights from range with the fireball. Passive fire DoT adds free damage while you reposition or swap items.
Counter: Ember Guard — 50% reduction
🌑

Dark

Shard: Echo Shard
Standard
ActiveSpawn a Shadow Clone to confuse and distract enemies
PassiveNearby hostile mobs ignore you — no aggro
PvP Tip: Shadow Clone in 1vX — enemies attack the clone while you deal real damage. Passive mob-ignore makes caving and Nether travel much safer.
Counter: Void Weave — 50% reduction
🌪️

Wind

Shard: Feather
Standard
ActiveAir Dash — burst forward at high speed
PassiveSlow Fall (zero fall damage) + mid-air control
PvP Tip: Best escape tool. Jump off a cliff, activate dash mid-air, glide to safety. Also great for chasing fleeing players.
Counter: Frost Plate — 50% reduction
❄️

Ice

Shard: Prismarine Shard
Standard
ActiveIce Ray — freeze and slow the targeted enemy
PassiveEnemies nearby are permanently weakened (less attack)
PvP Tip: Hard CC — freeze enemies while companions and teammates pile on. Weakness aura makes you extremely tanky in group fights.
Counter: Frost Plate — 50% reduction
⚡

Lightning

Shard: Glowstone Dust
Standard
ActiveChain Lightning — jumps from mob to mob in range
PassiveHaste I — faster mining speed and attack rate
PvP Tip: Devastating in group fights — one activation hits multiple enemies. Haste I passive gives you faster attack speed than almost any other power.
Counter: Dragon Scale — 40% + 15 cap
🧳

Trader

Shard: Emerald
Standard
ActiveToggle invisibility for a few seconds
PassiveUnlock exclusive 1-emerald villager trades
PvP Tip: Invisibility active is perfect for ambushes. Activate before engaging from a base. Emerald passive makes you the wealthiest player through easy villager farming.
Counter: Dragon Scale — 40% + 15 cap
🐇

Rift

Shard: Rabbit Foot
Standard
ActiveBlink — instantly teleport 30 blocks in your look direction
PassiveSpeed I — always slightly faster than others
PvP Tip: Best gap-closer and escape combined. Blink through a wall to escape, or into melee range to engage. Speed I passive means you nearly always outrun chasers.
Counter: Void Weave — 50% reduction
🫀

Warden

Shard: Sculk Shrieker
Rare
ActiveSonic Boom — 25-block beam, bypasses armor, 14 damage (10s CD)
PassiveDarkness Aura on nearby mobs + 25% bonus void damage on every melee hit
PvP Tip: Sonic Boom bypasses all armor completely. Use it to finish players hiding in full netherite. Void damage passive means you always deal more than the tooltip shows.
Counter: Warden Carapace — 60% reduction
☠️

Wither

Shard: Nether Star
Rare
ActiveWither Barrage — 3 rays × 8 damage + Wither II effect (9s CD)
PassiveSoul Drain on melee + constant Wither I aura to nearby mobs
PvP Tip: Wither Barrage + Soul Drain = incredible sustain in long fights. Each ray applies Wither which ticks through armor. Great against tanky 20+ heart players.
Counter: Dragon Scale — 40% + 15 cap
CRAFT-ONLY · 2 POWERS

Legendary Powers

Cannot be obtained from the spin wheel — you must craft them. The hardest and most powerful on the server.

🐉

Dragon

Shard: Dragon's Breath — Craft Only (Hardest)
Legendary
Active 1Dragon Sovereignty — 8s transformation: 3× melee + KB immunity (15s CD)
Active 2Dimensional Collapse (Q key) — 30-block singularity pulls then blasts (30s CD)
PassiveVoid Wings (no fall dmg) + Dragon Rage auto-triggers at <25% HP
PvP Tip: Dimensional Collapse → Dragon Sovereignty combo is nearly unstoppable in 1v1. Dragon Rage at low HP means you become MORE dangerous when nearly dead.
Counter: Dragon Scale — 40% + 15 cap

Craft: collect all 9 shard materials → Super Energy Crystal → Dragon Shard

🌀

Gravity

Shard: Heavy Core — Craft Only
Legendary
ActiveSummon 5 Meteors — rocks rain from the sky on enemies
Passive3× melee damage + Slowness on hit
PvP Tip: 3× melee passive = highest raw DPS in the game, no activation needed. Slowness on hit makes escape nearly impossible. Use Meteor when enemies cluster. Credit: Zeref_A
Counter: Dragon Scale — 40% + 15 cap
