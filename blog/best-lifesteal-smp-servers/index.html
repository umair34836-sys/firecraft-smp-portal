What to Look For in Other Servers

Beyond FireCraft, other quality private Lifesteal SMPs share similar traits. When evaluating another server, use this checklist:

Check Discord activity before applying

A server with 200+ Discord members and messages in the last 24 hours is active. A server where the last message is from 2 weeks ago is effectively dead. Don't invest time applying to dead servers.

Look for established seasons, not brand-new launches

Servers that are in "Season 2" or "Season 3" have proven they can maintain a community and reset cleanly. Brand new servers launched this week are high-risk — most die within a month.

Custom plugins vs. public plugin packs

Some servers use off-the-shelf Lifesteal plugins available to anyone. Servers with custom-coded features (like FireCraft's power system) offer genuinely unique gameplay that you can't get elsewhere.

Staff responsiveness

Send a message in the Discord server's general channel asking a question about the server. How fast do staff or experienced players respond? Response time in Discord is a good proxy for how well the server is managed.
