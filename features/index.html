PLUGIN 1

⚡ Custom Powers

Every player spins a wheel on first join and receives a unique power as a Shard item. 10 standard powers are spin-eligible — 1 special (Gravity) and 2 legendary (Warden, Dragon) are craft-only. Powers grant an active ability (SNEAK + RIGHT CLICK) and a permanent passive. Kill someone in PvP and steal their Shard.

🎰

Spinning Wheel on First Join

A custom animation plays on first login — the wheel spins and lands on a random power. The Shard item appears in your inventory with an invisible PDC tag storing your power type and name. You cannot drop the Shard, but it drops to your killer on a PvP death (natural deaths return it to you on respawn).

⚔️ PvP Shard Mechanics

  • Killed by a player? Your Shard drops — the killer picks it up and gains your power.
  • Natural death (mobs, fall, fire, drowning)? Shard returns to you on respawn.
  • Looting is possible — a skilled fighter can hold multiple enemy Shards and switch powers.
  • Admin commands: /powers give, /powers reset, /powers respin, /weapon give

PLUGIN 2

🛡️ Custom Armor System

Five craftable armor sets — each designed to counter specific powers. Equip all 4 pieces for a full-set passive bonus. Combine sets strategically to build the ultimate counter-build.

⚖️

How Damage Reduction Works

Each armor piece reduces damage by (Full Set Reduction ÷ 4) per hit from its matched power. Multiple different sets stack multiplicatively. Dragon Scale hard caps all power damage at 15 per hit when wearing the full set.

PLUGIN 3

🐾 Companion System

Tame almost any Minecraft mob and turn it into a loyal companion. Companions level up, remember their owner, use special abilities, and persist across server restarts. Each player can have up to 3 companions at once.

🎯

How to Tame

Every mob has a specific Taming Item (e.g. Wolf = Bone). Hold the item, right-click the mob to start a taming session — a Boss Bar with a 30-second timer appears. Feed the mob the required number of times before the timer runs out. Success: the mob is named, loyal to you, and starts at Level 1.

📈

Level 1 → 100

Companions earn XP from every hit and kill. HP, attack, and speed grow by +3% per level. XP requirement caps after level 31 so grinding stays fun.

❤️

Loyalty System

Loyalty ranges 0–100, earned through combat. It decays 1 point every 5 minutes of inactivity. Unlocks future advanced features.

💾

Restart-Safe Persistence

All companion data (level, name, loyalty, mode) saves to plugins/CustomMobs/companions.yml. Companions re-spawn when you log back in.

🔄

Death & Respawn

If your companion dies, it respawns 5 minutes later with full stats intact. If you're offline the respawn waits until you reconnect.

🛡️

Trust System

Mark other players as trusted — companions won't attack them or their companions. Perfect for alliance play.

⚔️

Fight Mode

Toggle /companion fight on to allow your companions to attack enemy players' companions in PvP — off by default.

3 Behaviour Modes

🏃

Follow

Companion walks alongside you. Teleports to you if it falls more than 30 blocks behind.

🛡️

Guard

Stays at a fixed position. Attacks any hostile mob or enemy entering a 20-block radius.

🪑

Sit

Companion stays in place and does nothing — for safe storage in your base.
